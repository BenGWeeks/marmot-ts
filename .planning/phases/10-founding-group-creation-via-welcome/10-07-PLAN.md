<objective>
Close the CR-03 conformance hole recorded against 10-VERIFICATION.md truth 2 (FOUND-02, "PARTIAL"). The engine's
`case "foundingAdd"` checks only `groupData` presence and `mayPrepareLocalCommit`. Because `SendIntent` is public
through the `./engine` subpath, any caller can submit `foundingAdd` on a live group at epoch N: the engine stages
a commit that is never published, the caller is told to `confirmPublished()` immediately, and the local member
advances to N+1 through a commit no peer ever sees — a guaranteed silent fork. That violates
`refs/marmot/protocol-core/publish-lifecycle.md` line 77 ("The empty-obligation exception is limited to the epoch-0
creation and, when applicable, its immediately following founding Add Commit"). MDK is structurally safe because
its founding Add is internal to `do_create_group`; marmot-ts exposes it, so the invariant must be enforced in the
case itself.

Per the user's 2026-09-28 locked decision: reject (throw before `createCommit`) unless epoch is 0, the local
member is the sole leaf, there are no unapplied proposals, and every proposal in the intent is an Add — with an
engine test per rejection branch asserting state is unchanged.

Output: the guard in `group-engine.ts`, documented preconditions on the `foundingAdd` `SendIntent`, and five
rejection tests.
</objective>
